02 / REVIEW ONE REAL TASK

Introduce a guest, then change their title.

1

Choose & bind

Select Logo accent. Create a hidden cue. Choose the exact current subject; source name/title overrides and explicit blanks remain available.

2

Build privately

Edit text, style and placement. Open Design layers for text boxes, groups, images, fills and gradients. Compare and Undo remain local.

3

Cue, then Show

Cue accepts a complete revision on Preview. Show puts that Cue on Program after native preparation. Editing a later draft cannot change the accepted Cue.

4

Update & recover

Change the title, then Update on air. Hide or Clear. Save and restore hidden; explicitly rebind sources and re-arm automation.

Review question: can you tell, at every step, what is private, what is cued, and what is actually on Program?

04 / PRESERVATION & ACCEPTANCE

Keep the wins. Make the gaps visible.

PRESERVE

Existing production behavior

  • All three legacy looks, saved appearances, source naming and intentionally blank secondary lines.
  • Scene/Tiles layers, transforms, grades and scopes-only ROI.
  • Captions and the webcam-only OH framer/LUFS overlay retain their separate controls.
  • Source ISOs stay clean. Graphics do not alter recording, stream destinations, camera registration or audio settings.
  • Old/new lower-third authorities never render the same family together.
STILL TO QUALIFY

This is not a finished beta claim

  • Remaining package/logo repair, saved workflow and migration integration.
  • Qualified automation, manual priority and the director-to-native execution trace.
  • Complete cue-rack polish and end-to-end native UI workflow review.
  • Enabled-output pixels and scheduled deadlines, packaged launch, and sustained real-meeting acceptance.
  • Windows evidence does not establish Metal/macOS or physical-display continuity.

The installed app has not been replaced. Your design feedback will shape the complete review candidate; no issue closes from approving this webpage.
