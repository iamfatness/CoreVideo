What this changes—and what it keeps

Kept in the overview

Ten stable slots, category/type, observed format and FPS, signal, audio summary, quick MV and separate ISO state. Secondary columns are customizable; important data is not replaced by a big preview.

Opened on demand

The selected input’s larger preview, assignment, lower-third text and detailed controls. Close the inspector to recover the full-width table. Selection and filtering never reassign a source.

Same production workflow

Sources assigns inputs and MV inclusion. Routing owns output routes; Scenes owns composition and Take. Detailed grade, audio, recording and webcam-only overlays remain their existing workflows.

At narrower widths the inspector stacks below the table. Secondary columns move into row Details so Format/FPS, MV and ISO remain available without horizontal page scrolling.
