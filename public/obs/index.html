CoreVideo for OBS

Bring Zoom participants into OBS as native video and audio sources. Build scenes from individual guests, screen shares, and interpretation feeds.

Record individual feedsRecord selected feeds as per-participant MP4s with isolated audio alongside your OBS Program recording.
